# v0.3 — validating Muse Exec 1.4.0-R4302.1 for the Reviewer binding

Labels: **LIVE-OBSERVED** (the one authorized real turn, PASS, validated independently), **MECHANICALLY ENFORCED**,
**FAKE-PROCESS PROVEN**.

Outcome in one line: the installed **Muse Exec 1.4.0-R4302.1** passed **one real fresh-review turn** as the Reviewer under
`V0.3-MUSE-R4302-REVIEWER`, and the independent review of its evidence passed **100 of 100 checks**.
- **Validated scope:** 1.4.0-R4302.1 is validated for **exactly the Reviewer binding on exactly its binary** (SHA-256
  `61dbb475…b14ac`), next to the unchanged O5.5B24 validation of 1.4.0-R4161.1. Nothing wider.
- **The Explorer binding stays unvalidated.** The validated Reviewer binding keeps serving as the exploration transport.
- **Authorization:** `V0.3-MUSE-R4302-REVIEWER` is consumed.
- **Effect:** the v0.3 live acceptance's Reviewer precondition is met on the maintainer's machine; the runner still checks
  it from the real `doctor --probe` report before any model turn.

## Why

On 2026-09-27 (21:45 UTC) Muse updated itself on the maintainer's machine.

- **The swap.** `muse-bin-1.4.0-R4302.1.exe` replaced `muse-bin-1.4.0-R4161.1.exe`, and `.muse-version` now selects
  1.4.0-R4302.1.
- **Why the Reviewer was refused.** Fusion's Reviewer validation (O5.5B24) is bound to the release 1.4.0-R4161.1 **and**
  its binary's SHA-256. Until this validation, Fusion therefore correctly refused the new binary: the Reviewer's
  read-only posture read `unknown`, and no investigation or review ran on it.
- **The consequence.** The v0.3 live acceptance stopped at its precondition, before any model turn.

Nothing is downgraded and no 1.4.x range is admitted. This milestone validates the one installed binary, or not.

## What is validated, exactly

A second `BindingValidation` (`src/runtime/provider-profiles.ts`) next to O5.5B24's records that Muse Exec `1.4.0-R4302.1`
is validated only for:
- the **Reviewer** role, `muse-spark-1.3`, effort `low`;
- `provider meta`, `maxModelSteps 4`, `malformedOutputRetries 0`;
- the binary `muse-bin-1.4.0-R4302.1.exe` with SHA-256 `61dbb475…b14ac`.

`reviewerLiveRecords()` appends the V0.3-R4302 PASS; the O5.5B24 record is unchanged. Enforcement is O5.5B24's, unchanged
(see `docs/o5-5b24-muse14-reviewer-live.md` §3): the registry hands an exactly matching binding each release's own SHA-256,
and the adapter claims the launch-flag facts only when the executable about to run has exactly that SHA-256.

**Not generalized:**
- 1.4.0-R4161.1 keeps its own validation on its own binary; neither entry covers the other's bytes.
- `isValidatedRuntimeVersion` stays false for both 1.4 releases; the transport-wide validated releases stay
  `["1.3.0-R3401.1"]`.
- Every other case stays unverified:
  - the **Explorer** binding (its role, and the default Explorer binding with no step limit and the default retry);
  - another model, effort, step budget, retry policy or provider;
  - the Muse Change Author and the MSP transport;
  - another binary under the same name;
  - any other or future release (`1.4.0-R4303.1`, `1.4.1-R4302.1`, `1.4.0-R4302.2`, …).
